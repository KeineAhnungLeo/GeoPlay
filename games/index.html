Our Games

We currently offer three main game modes. Each mode includes all countries and can be customized as needed. Additional game modes may be added in the future.

Guess the flag

In the “Guess the Flag” game, you're shown a flag and asked to match it to the correct country. You'll be given a choice of several countries, one of which is the correct one for the flag.

Guess the Top-Level-Domain

In “Guess the Top-Level-Domain”, you need to match the country code to the correct country. For example, these codes appear at the end of a web address (www.xyz.us).

Guess the capital

In “Guess the Capital,” you have to choose which country a capital city belongs to. It also works the other way around, where you have to match the capital city to its country.
